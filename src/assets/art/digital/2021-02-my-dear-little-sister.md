My dear little sister,

They say you're a grown-up.  
I see you dancing for the Powerpuff Girls.  
They say you are a woman now.  
A shawl above your head  
To guard you from the evils on the street..  
I see you draping it on like a throne.  
Of course you look beautiful.  
They make you sit inside a room.  
For how long can they?  
Till you become a warrior?

I see you find solace in cartoons.  
Maybe Doofensmith should make a warriorator..  
They fill you up with sweets.  
“How sweet of them!”  
I see you in aww with the  
Red henna on your hands.  
Of course you look beautiful..

I see them burning the dress you cherished.  
I see you unable to even fold it properly.  
I see them checking their bank balance.  
I see you preparing for a ceremony.  
What exactly is celebrated  
A bit of a doubt for me. They say  
“It’s all to make you feel normal”.  
“Totally normal!” I say.

I see them painting the walls  
What? Are you a virus??  
I see people talking  
"Maybe it's enough gold  
Would we get it back?"  
I see you waiting to meet  
All your life's friends  
And have a hearty chat  
About that pretty dress you lost.

Like all annoying little sisters  
I see you enjoy all the sweets  
Leaving all the bitterness for me.  
While I find little happiness  
In your ignorance.
