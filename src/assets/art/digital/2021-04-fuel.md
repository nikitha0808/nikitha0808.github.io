“People’s memories are maybe the fuel they burn to stay alive. Whether those memories have any importance or not, it doesn’t matter as far as the maintenance of life is concerned. They’re all just fuel. “

I read this book called After dark by Haruki Murakami. These lines made me think how I have been sleeping an extra hour in the mornings these days just to dream and how it’s mostly about my school,college and all my friends.  Maybe it’s the actual fuel!

But usually i also get nicely from my mom for sleeping late. That’s an added fuel!!😂
