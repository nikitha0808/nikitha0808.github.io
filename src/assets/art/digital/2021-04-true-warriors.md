It’s very depressing to watch the news, the social media and the government these days. I can’t imagine the trauma every doctor out there fighting the virus has to go through. Empty cylinders of oxygen, remdesivir, lifeless humans, the requests and cries.. Being right there but unable to do anything and see people die...

I just feel grateful to every doctors sailing through this pandemic, giving hopes and lives to millions. True warriors indeed.

Let’s all stay home and help them in the easiest way we can. It’s the best self help as well. I hope all of us get through this pandemic in good health soon!  
Take care❤️
