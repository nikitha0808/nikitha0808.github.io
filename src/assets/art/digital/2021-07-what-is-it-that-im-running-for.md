Is it the sky or the ground?  
The petrichor or the bathed newborns, Paddy I mean!  
Is it that old clay house at  
the end of this narrow path,  
The highest bridge in the  
Ants’ kingdom.  
Is it the chickens that await  
Their everyday grains to munch?  
Is it that tall coconut tree  
Celebrating it’s reach for high winds?  
Or is it the other short ones  
Watching my grandma  
Sit in their shadow and weave  
Mats outta their withered relatives  
What is it that I’m running for??
