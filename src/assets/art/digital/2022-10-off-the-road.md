Off the road

Shy sun rays peek through the branches. ‘Look up, step out and breathe in!’ they say. I wait a second. And a couple more. Isn’t it always safer inside? For once, some part of me disagrees. The one that beats. My hands give in and I reach for the button. The panel above grumbles in protest. Well, I did call for a surprise workout. I continue to press down the button even when it’s all open. As if doing just that would make the next steps happen. The wind is quick to enter, bringing with it the smell of moss, flowers and petrichor. The beats quicken, winning over other parts of me. My legs break faith next and I rise from my seat. My hair ditches me to befriend the brazen breeze. Unlike me, it doesn’t think twice to join a dance. The beating organ now pounds. I hear chirps nearby, tiny cheerleaders egging me on. I don’t see the birds themselves, but suddenly there’re wings all around. I don’t hear the sharp honks or my flooding inbox. I reach out and grab the wings. All of me wants out of the race as I embrace the invite to get off the road..
