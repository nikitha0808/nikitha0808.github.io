Sometimes we never know how much we like a hobby but we just get into doing it randomly and get consumed by it for days. Here's one such cover art I designed for @suryasrini.music single Oonjalaadalaam!

With countless creative blocks, design changes and late nighters, this has by far been the most exhausting but fulfilling piece I have worked on. I will never forget the pitch dark Anjapar with heavy rains and just 1% charge on iPad and my desperation to put together the polygons of her mind😂. Now that the final piece is here, the amount of satisfaction it gives is just indescribable!! It was a joyful experience!😁 Thanks for trusting me with this one @suryasrini.music and wishing you and the team great success!😁❤️
